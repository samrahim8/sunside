Neighborhoods of Horizon West

Six distinct villages, each with its own character. Here's what makes each one unique.

Lakeside Village

The established one
$387K – $1.1M+

Lakeside Village Center & Westside Shoppes — largest commercial hub in HW

Mature landscaping with established neighborhoods. The first village (1997), offering extensive shopping, dining, and services within the community itself.

HOA: $130–$250/qtr Units: 8,915 → Families seeking convenience

Village of Bridgewater

The family favorite
$350K – $700K

Parks and playgrounds in every neighborhood

Most populated village with family-friendly design and main street-style businesses. The largest by capacity with 10,450 approved units.

HOA: $130–$280/qtr Units: 10,450 → Budget-conscious families & first-time buyers

Town Center

The walkable one
$450K – $1M+

Waterfront dining, PopStroke, Cinépolis + Lake Hancock boardwalk

Urban energy within a suburb. Hamlin and Silver Leaf together span 3,624 acres with 2M+ sq ft of commercial space, farmers markets, and community events.

HOA: $150–$300/qtr Units: 4,870 → Young professionals & foodies

Village F

The nature lover
$325K – $650K

The Mark shopping district + Disney fireworks views from home

Lake views, nature trails, and a quieter pace while maintaining walkability and convenient access to amenities.

HOA: $100–$250/qtr Units: 3,891 → Nature lovers & Disney Cast Members

Village H

The upscale newcomer
$430K – $900K+

Hickory Nut Lake + proximity to Flamingo Crossings Town Center

Newer construction with spacious single-family homes and townhomes, featuring upscale and private neighborhoods with strong community identity.

HOA: $130–$300/qtr Units: 6,548 → Newer construction seekers near Disney

Village I (Ovation)

The nature-forward one
$400K – $800K

Nature-forward design + direct Disney access via Western Way

The final village with unified master developer vision emphasizing conservation zones, kayaking, and birdwatching integrated into neighborhood design.

HOA: $100–$250/qtr Units: TBD → New construction & nature enthusiasts
